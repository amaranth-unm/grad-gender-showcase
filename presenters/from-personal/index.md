## Reifying Queer Consciousness

In this article review, it is shown that Queer methodology and Queer consciousness align with forms of consciousness that aren't as well known or supported by academia, which raises many questions. This research is a literature review on peer reviewed studies involving Queer consciousness as well as concepts of consciousness from the field of psychology. Through Queer labeling and methodology there is a possible comparison to Maxine Sheets-Johnstone's work on consciousness. In using "unpopular" concepts of consciousness, a guise of capitalist hierarchy is lifted and revealed as the negative resource that it is. Concepts of "Higher Order Thought" (HOT) condone the expression of complexity only after checking a previous "box" of conceptual "understanding." HOT is something where those with disorganized speech, or a child, do not qualify as using consciousness. Through this hierarchy of expression found in HOT there is a hierarchy of only a possibility of expression. Yet through Sheets-Johnstoneâ€™s work and similar there is a recognition that things are not hierarchies that are deserving of worth, there are understandings using a whole body of experience as a method to consciousness.  

Using Queer methodology to recognize that the boundaries of words and expression are more fluid than strict boxes or HOT, there is discovery of oneâ€™s own method of understanding. There is a realization of a method of understanding that can encompass the words and unique minds of the neurodivergent, minorities, Queer folks, and even allies. The structure of HOT seems to condone the use of systematic alienation of minority groups from their very existence. Habitual disinclusion is a profound character flaw. Words and constructs of thought processes from minority groups are powerful creations that should be considered valuable to even the majority population. Through acceptance found in Queer consciousness we can better understand ourselves as well as others.
